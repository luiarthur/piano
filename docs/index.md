Hi, I'm Arthur.

I live in Los Alamos and offer private weekly jazz piano lessons to
students (young and old) that are already comforatble with all 12 major scales,
and preferrably all 12 harmonic/melodic minor scales (two octaves up and down at
$\ge$ 72 bpm).

I have played classical piano for about 30 years and jazz for about 15 years. 
Learning jazz is different from learning classical piano. In addition to
technique, jazz requires familiarity with and application of music theory.
Learning/playing jazz has, for me, opened wider the door to musical
understanding, expression, creativity, spontaneity, and fun. 

For students that meet the above prerequisites, I begin by introducint jazz
keyboard harmony. Going through this material takes about 3-6 months. By the end
of this, students will have learned the basics of how to read jazz [lead
sheets][1], short music forms common in jazz, and how to play (or work out)
accompaniments on the left hand and the written melodies on the right hand. Jazz
keyboard harmony is straightforward in the sense that it can be formulaic. It is
something you can practice and master. Though, developing "your own sound" can
take years.

After several months of harmony, we begin exploring topics like transcribing
music, improvising (soloing), writing jazz arrangements. 

I work at LANL full time (as a statistician), so can take students only on
weekdays between 6PM and 8PM.

If you would like to take lessons from me, please email me at
[luiarthur@gmail.com](luiarthur@gmail.com).
